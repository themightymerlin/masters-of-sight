About Masters Of Sight

Masters Of Sight is a curated sanctuary for the visually obsessed. A place where craft is worshipped, where every frame is a deliberate act, and where the boundary between art and entertainment ceases to exist. This is not a portfolio. This is a resource for those who demand better.

Why This Was Created

I built this because the world is drowning in content and forgetting what vision actually looks like. Consider this a reminder.

My name is Benny Jackson. I built this on my own time, with my own taste, because no one was going to build it for me. That's kind of the whole point.

For the ones who can't look away from great work. You know who you are.

Designer

Herb Lubalin

Typography / Editorial American 1918 - 1981

Herb Lubalin treated typography as architecture and emotion simultaneously. Working in New York through the 1960s and 70s, he transformed the typeface from a neutral carrier of language into a charged, expressive visual event. His magazine designs for Eros, Fact, and Avant Garde pushed typography to its conceptual limits, and the Avant Garde typeface he designed with Tom Carnase became one of the most recognisable and influential letterforms of the twentieth century.

Signature Traits

  • Letters treated as interlocking architectural forms.
  • Negative space between letterforms used as active compositional element.
  • Editorial design as manifesto. Every cover a statement.
  • Ligatures and custom letterfit pushed to their structural limits.
  • Typography and image fused rather than arranged alongside each other.

Major Works

  • Avant Garde magazine (1968–1971)

    The most visually adventurous magazine in American history. Every issue a typographic event.

  • ITC Avant Garde Gothic (1970)

    The typeface derived from the magazine's logo. Still everywhere, still electrifying when used correctly.

  • Mother & Child logotype

    Two words. Perfectly letterspaced. The infant tucked inside the ampersand. Still the most elegant piece of visual wordplay ever made.

  • Herb Lubalin: American Graphic Designer (Monograph)

    Adrian Shaughnessy's survey of a career built almost entirely on letterforms, tracing the path from Avant Garde magazine through decades of typographic experiments most designers only encounter in fragments.
