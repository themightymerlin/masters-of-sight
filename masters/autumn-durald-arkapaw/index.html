About Masters Of Sight

Masters Of Sight is a curated sanctuary for the visually obsessed. A place where craft is worshipped, where every frame is a deliberate act, and where the boundary between art and entertainment ceases to exist. This is not a portfolio. This is a resource for those who demand better.

Why This Was Created

I built this because the world is drowning in content and forgetting what vision actually looks like. Consider this a reminder.

My name is Benny Jackson. I built this on my own time, with my own taste, because no one was going to build it for me. That's kind of the whole point.

For the ones who can't look away from great work. You know who you are.

Cinematographer

Autumn Durald Arkapaw

Naturalistic / Large-Format American 1979

Autumn Durald Arkapaw shoots the American South, the multiverse, and teenage yearning with the same unblinking clarity. She built her name in intimate 35mm coming of age work before Marvel pulled her into Loki and then Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, proving the instinct scaled. On Ryan Coogler's Sinners she shot IMAX 65mm and Ultra Panavision, formats no woman had touched before her, and in 2026 became the first woman and first person of color to win the Academy Award for Best Cinematography. Her frames never announce themselves. They just feel inevitable, whether she is lighting a vampire blues bar in 1930s Mississippi or a Steadicam glide through a Tesseract courtroom.

Signature Traits

  • Shoots on 35mm, 65mm, and IMAX film whenever the budget allows, treating celluloid as a texture choice rather than a nostalgia move.
  • Steadicam and handheld work built for continuous emotional momentum, rarely cutting away from a performance mid breath.
  • Warm practical light pushed against cold reflective sources to mark cultural or supernatural turns within a single scene.
  • Equally fluent at nine figure blockbuster scale and two million dollar indie intimacy, without changing her actual toolkit.
  • Repeat collaboration over one off assignments, building sustained visual languages with the same directors across years.

Major Works

  • Sinners (2025)

    Ryan Coogler's vampire blues epic, shot on IMAX 65mm film and Ultra Panavision, a first for a woman on both formats. Won her the Best Cinematography Oscar.

  • Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2022)

    The Disney+ debut that pulled her into the MCU, real ceilings on set and in camera effects over greenscreen. Earned her an Emmy nomination for the Lamentis episode.
