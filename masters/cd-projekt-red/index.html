About Masters Of Sight

Masters Of Sight is a curated sanctuary for the visually obsessed. A place where craft is worshipped, where every frame is a deliberate act, and where the boundary between art and entertainment ceases to exist. This is not a portfolio. This is a resource for those who demand better.

Why This Was Created

I built this because the world is drowning in content and forgetting what vision actually looks like. Consider this a reminder.

My name is Benny Jackson. I built this on my own time, with my own taste, because no one was going to build it for me. That's kind of the whole point.

Dream Maker

CD Projekt Red

Dark Fantasy / Cyberpunk Noir Polish Founded 1994

CD Projekt Red was founded in Warsaw in 1994 by two friends who started out importing and localizing Western games for the Polish market before deciding to build their own. That path shaped everything that followed, a Polish studio steeped in Andrzej Sapkowski's Witcher novels, translating literary fantasy into a trilogy that argued player choice could carry the weight of real moral consequence. Cyberpunk 2077 arrived in 2020 as a catastrophe of its own making, a broken launch on last generation consoles that nearly cost the studio its reputation. Then something rare happened, years of patient rework, the 2.0 update, and the Phantom Liberty expansion turned public failure into one of gaming's most complete redemption arcs, helped along by an anime tie in that introduced Night City to an audience that never touched a controller. Few studios have shipped both a genuine disaster and a genuine masterpiece under the same name and lived to tell a coherent story about it.

Signature Traits

  • Moral choice systems built without a clean karma meter. Consequences surface hours or games later, often too subtle to trace back to the decision that caused them.
  • Literary source material treated as canon, not commodity. Sapkowski's tone and politics preserved rather than smoothed for a wider audience.
  • Post launch redemption as a house habit. Cyberpunk 2077's 2.0 overhaul and Phantom Liberty expansion rebuilt a broken game into one of the decade's most acclaimed.
  • Free DLC bundled generously into every release, a policy that turned goodwill into part of the brand itself.
  • World detail treated as worldview. Night City's advertising, architecture, and slang all argue the same thesis about unchecked capitalism.

Major Works

  • Cyberpunk 2077 (2020)

    A launch disaster on last gen consoles rebuilt over three years into Night City's fullest version. The 2.0 update and Phantom Liberty expansion turned a punchline into one of the best RPGs of its generation.

  • The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (2015)

    Geralt hunting his adopted daughter across a war torn continent while every side quest carries the moral weight of the main one. Still the genre's high water mark for choice with real consequence.

  • The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings (2011)

    A branching second act that diverges so completely between paths that players compared notes to realize they'd played different games. The moment CD Projekt Red proved Witcher wasn't a one game studio.

  • The Witcher (2007)

    A regicide mystery built from Sapkowski's novels, unpolished but unmistakably the studio's founding argument, that a video game could carry literary weight.
