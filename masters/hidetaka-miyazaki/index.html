About Masters Of Sight

Masters Of Sight is a curated sanctuary for the visually obsessed. A place where craft is worshipped, where every frame is a deliberate act, and where the boundary between art and entertainment ceases to exist. This is not a portfolio. This is a resource for those who demand better.

Why This Was Created

I built this because the world is drowning in content and forgetting what vision actually looks like. Consider this a reminder.

My name is Benny Jackson. I built this on my own time, with my own taste, because no one was going to build it for me. That's kind of the whole point.

For the ones who can't look away from great work. You know who you are.

Dream Maker

Hidetaka Miyazaki

Dark / Mythological Japanese 1974

Hidetaka Miyazaki didn't invent the action RPG. He just made everyone who came before him look like they were merely sketching the idea. As president of FromSoftware and creator of Demon's Souls, Dark Souls, Bloodborne, and Elden Ring, he built a new grammar for interactive storytelling, one where the world itself is the narrator, where death is a teaching tool rather than a punishment, and where lore is buried so deep in item descriptions and environmental detail that players have spent years excavating it. His games don't tell you what to feel. They construct a world so coherent, so oppressively beautiful, that feeling becomes involuntary.

Signature Traits

  • Environmental storytelling. Architecture, lighting, and enemy placement as narrative language.
  • Deliberate obscurity. Lore withheld, fragmented, buried, forcing the player to become an archaeologist.
  • Death as design. Failure reframed as progression, suffering reframed as mastery.
  • World cohesion over world explanation. Every corner of every map feels inhabited by a history you never fully learn.
  • Boss design as emotional climax. Each encounter a concentrated expression of the world's themes.

Major Works

  • Dark Souls Remastered (2011)

    The one that rewired expectations for an entire generation of game makers and players. A dying world held together by fire, faith, and the stubbornness of the undead. Its influence on everything that followed is immeasurable.

    PS4/PS5 Xbox Steam

  • Bloodborne (2015)

    Victorian gothic horror meets Lovecraftian cosmic dread. Faster, more aggressive, more terrifying. The most purely atmospheric world he has ever built.

    PS4/PS5

  • Elden Ring (2022)

    An open world designed by a man who understands that discovery is its own reward. Co-created with George R.R. Martin. Won Game of the Year. Sold 25 million copies. The mainstream finally caught up.

    PS4/PS5 Xbox Steam
