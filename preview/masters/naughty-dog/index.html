Dream Maker

Naughty Dog

Cinematic / Emotional Santa Monica, CA Founded 1984

Naughty Dog started making games about a marsupial in a mask and ended up with HBO on the phone. That arc is not an accident, it is the story of a studio that never stopped pushing against the ceiling of what the medium was supposed to be capable of. Founded in 1984 by Andy Gavin and Jason Rubin, acquired by Sony in 2001, and eventually home to Neil Druckmann and a generation of storytellers who believed games could carry the same emotional weight as the finest literature and film. They were right. The Last of Us did not just win awards. It changed the conversation about interactive storytelling permanently and then crossed over into television and changed that conversation too.

Signature Traits

  • Cinematic production values treated as a baseline, not an aspiration.
  • Character and relationship as the engine of every game. Spectacle always in service of story.
  • Performance capture elevated to genuine acting. Ashley Johnson, Troy Baker, Pedro Pascal.
  • The willingness to devastate the player emotionally as an act of respect, not cruelty.
  • A twenty year creative evolution from platformer to the most emotionally ambitious studio in the medium.

Major Works

  • Crash Bandicoot (1996)

    The game that put Naughty Dog on the map and helped define the PlayStation 1 era. Technical ambition and pure playfulness in equal measure.

  • Uncharted 2: Among Thieves (2009)

    The moment the industry understood what Naughty Dog was actually capable of. Cinematic action storytelling that made every other studio raise its ambitions.

  • The Last of Us (2013)

    A fungal apocalypse as the backdrop for the most emotionally precise story the medium has ever told. The game that made HBO call.

  • The Last of Us Part II (2020)

    The most courageously constructed sequel in gaming. Deliberately devastating. Refuses every comfort the first game offered. Won Game of the Year.
