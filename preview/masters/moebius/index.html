Dream Maker

Moebius (Jean Giraud)

Sci-Fi / Illustration French 1938 - 2012

Moebius is the source code of visual science fiction. Jean Giraud, working under that name from the 1970s onward, created comics of such hallucinatory visual richness that they rewired the imagination of every filmmaker, designer, and artist who encountered them, Ridley Scott hired him for Alien, Lucas consulted him. The visual language of Blade Runner, The Fifth Element, Dune, and virtually every significant sci-fi film of the past four decades flows directly from his line. He drew worlds that had never existed and made them feel like they had always been there.

Signature Traits

  • Line work of impossible precision and variation, thin to thick in a single stroke.
  • Vast open landscapes that feel simultaneously alien and primordially familiar.
  • Colour used with the restraint of a master watercolourist.
  • Narrative that prioritises wonder over plot.
  • The page treated as a window, not a flat surface.

Major Works

  • The Incal (1980–1988, with Jodorowsky)

    The greatest science fiction comic ever made. A cosmological epic that influenced everything.

  • Arzach (1975)

    Wordless. Pure image. A pterodactyl and a silent rider in alien landscapes. Revolutionary.

  • Alien, Tron, The Fifth Element (production design)

    His fingerprints on the visual DNA of cinema's most influential sci-fi productions.
